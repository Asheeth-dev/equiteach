# EquiTeach — 2–3 Minute Demo Script

**0:00 — Problem (landing page)**
Say: "Teacher hiring and reviews in schools come down to a 20-minute demo lesson and gut feel. Charisma wins, and good quiet teachers lose."
Point at the Problem / Solution / Different strip.

**0:20 — Solution (setup card)**
Keep "Deepa Raman · Class 8 · Science". Click **In-service review** (point out the hint "Detailed coaching plan"). Click **Begin guided observation**.
Say: "EquiTeach scores 10 visible classroom behaviors against written anchors."

**0:40 — Technology (observation screen)**
On metric 1, click the anchor **3** ("Includes one guided activity…"). Point at the right panel: projected score and tier update live.
Say: "Every score maps to a written behavior anchor, so two observers reach the same number. Scoring runs the same way every time — no AI guessing."
Press key **4** to show keyboard scoring, then click **Demo: fill a sample observation** ("to save time on stage").

**1:10 — Impact (results)**
Click **Complete evaluation**. Expected: **33 / 50 · Developing · "One focused practice cycle"**, coaching focus **Real-Time Comprehension**.
Show the marker on the tier scale, then the amber-highlighted Comprehension tile.
Say: "The head teacher doesn't get a score and nothing else. They get the single behavior to work on, plus a scripted coaching conversation."
Scroll to "What to strengthen next": the 8-sentence observer narrative (it names the strengths, the widest gap, why it matters and the one change to make), the three improvement cards, the four-step practice cycle and strengths.

**2:00 — Differentiation (USP moment)**
Click **Export PDF report**.
Say: "Same scores, same result, every time. That makes it defensible in hiring and fair in reviews. And every score ends with a next step for the teacher."

**2:20 — Close**
Optional: click **New observation**, switch to **Recruitment demo** to show the hire / conditional / decline verdict.
